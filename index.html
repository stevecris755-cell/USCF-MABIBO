About USCF Mabibo Associates

Who we are, and what "Social Welfare" means for this family.

Who We Are

USCF Mabibo Associates is a fellowship of alumni who once walked together as members of the University of Dar es Salaam Christian Fellowship (USCF) at Mabibo. Though we have since graduated and scattered across different cities, careers and seasons of life, we remain one family — bound not by proximity, but by the faith, friendships and memories we built together.

What is "Social Welfare"?

Social Welfare simply means the wellbeing of every member of this family — practically, emotionally, socially and spiritually. It is the commitment we make to look out for one another even after campus life ends. The Social Welfare Committee exists to carry out this commitment in five practical ways:

1. Financial Support

We contribute together so that no member walks through an emergency, illness, loss or major life event alone. Every contribution recorded in this app builds a fund we can lean on together.

2. Emotional & Spiritual Care

We show up for each other — in grief and in joy, at weddings, births, hospital visits and hard seasons. Testimonies shared in "What I've Learned" remind us we are not walking alone.

3. Staying Connected

Distance and busy schedules can quietly pull people apart. Announcements, events and this member directory exist to keep us in touch, no matter which city or country we now call home.

4. Growing Together

We share skills, opportunities, mentorship and career advice with one another — because the same community that shaped us in faith can also help us grow professionally.

5. Transparency & Trust

Every contribution, event and decision is kept visible to all members. Good stewardship of what we build together is itself an act of love and respect for this family.

Our Foundation

"Let us not neglect our meeting together, as some people do, but encourage one another." — Hebrews 10:25

Everything we do — the giving, the gathering, the checking-in — flows from the same faith that first brought us together at Mabibo. This app is simply a tool to help us live that out more faithfully, wherever life has taken us.

Admin Notes

Admin sign-in now uses a real Firebase account (email + password), managed in Firebase Console → Authentication. To change the Admin password, use "Forgot password?" on the Sign In screen with the Admin email, or reset it directly in Firebase Console. Member passwords are their own — you can send them a password reset email from their profile in Members, or they can use "Change My Password" once signed in.
